Our Story

Our project began with a simple observation: stress has become one of the most common health challenges today, affecting concentration, emotional balance, and long-term well-being. Many people only realize how stressed they are when symptoms become severe, and most available solutions either depend on medication or require constant manual effort. We wanted to design a system that could detect stress early and respond automatically.

The idea was to create a closed-loop biofeedback system that continuously monitors physiological signals from the body, interprets them using machine learning, and applies a safe, non-pharmacological intervention to help restore balance. We focused on two important signals: brain activity and heart activity, because they reflect how the nervous system responds to stress in real time.

In our system, sensors measure these physiological signals and send them to a processing unit. Machine learning algorithms analyze the patterns and determine whether the person is entering a stressed state. Instead of only displaying the data, the system takes action: it activates transcutaneous vagus nerve stimulation, which helps activate the parasympathetic nervous system, promoting relaxation and reducing stress.

To make the system practical and user-friendly, we developed a real-time monitoring interface that allows stress levels to be visualized continuously. This provides both feedback to the user and validation of the system's effect, showing how stress levels decrease after intervention.

The final result is a complete closed-loop solution: detection, analysis, intervention, and monitoring, all working together automatically. Our project demonstrates how engineering, physiology, and artificial intelligence can be combined to create technology that not only measures the human body but actively helps improve well-being.
